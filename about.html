I design the screens and experiences where people meet a service — in apps, on the web, and in physical spaces. At Petnow I led the brand strategy team through a rebrand (iF Design Award 2024), the core app flow, and the screen specs and design system. At Hyperconnect I make marketing creative and use AI tools to change how it gets made. On the side, I’m building two games with AI.
